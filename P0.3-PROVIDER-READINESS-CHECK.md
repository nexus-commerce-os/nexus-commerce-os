## 1. ⚠️ The finding that should shape the account decision

**Decision 2 requires "Shipping included for ZIP `<ZIP>`". Most affiliate product feeds do not carry
destination-specific shipping cost.**

Affiliate catalogue feeds typically expose price, currency, availability, identifiers and a
deep-link. Shipping is commonly absent, or present only as a flat/free-shipping flag with no ZIP
sensitivity — because the affiliate network is not the merchant's checkout and often does not compute
destination shipping at all.

If that holds for the selected provider, then under §5 of the approved design **every offer has an
incomplete price claim, nothing is rankable, and the slice produces an empty best-offer result.** The
design would be behaving correctly and the increment would still fail to demonstrate anything.

This is a **contradiction between Decision 2 and the data affiliate networks actually supply**, and it
must be resolved *before* an account is chosen, because it changes which provider is suitable.

**Three ways out, for your ruling (§6, decision 1):**

| Option | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Make shipping-cost data a hard selection criterion** | Only accept a provider/merchant set that exposes ZIP-sensitive shipping, or free-shipping-to-US as an authoritative flag | Narrows provider choice sharply; may eliminate both Impact and CJ for this category |
| **B. Narrow to free-shipping-eligible offers only** | Shipping is then *known* and equals zero, so the all-in claim is honest and rankable | Smallest change, keeps the honesty rule intact; shrinks the result set |
| **C. Rank on item price, labelled "excluding shipping"** | Weakens the all-in premise the product is built on | **I do not recommend this** — "best all-in price" is the core claim |

**I recommend B**: free-shipping-eligible, US-destination, new-condition over-ear headphones. Shipping
is genuinely known (zero), the ZIP still governs *eligibility*, the honesty rule is preserved, and the
walking skeleton proves the whole path. The result set is smaller, which for a thin slice is a
feature.

## 4. Data-capability questionnaire — answer from the real account

For **over-ear headphones, US, new condition** specifically. Category-level answers are not
transferable; a feed rich in apparel can be thin in audio.

**Identity** — Is GTIN/UPC/EAN present? **What percentage of records in this category carry one?**
Coverage decides whether §9's exact-match-only rule yields a usable catalogue or quarantines most of
it. Is there a provider-canonical product id? Are brand and model separate fields?

**Price** — Currency. Is it merchant-current or periodically synced, and at what cadence? Is there an
observation timestamp? Are sale and list prices distinguished?

**Shipping (§1)** — Is any shipping cost exposed? Is it ZIP-sensitive? Is there a reliable
free-shipping flag? Is US-destination eligibility explicit?

**Availability** — In-stock signal? Condition field, and can NEW be filtered authoritatively?

**Operational** — Rate limits (per second/day). Sandbox or test mode? Deep-link mechanism and whether
a sub-id can carry our `click_id`. Postback mode (real-time or batch). Cache duration permitted,
price-display restrictions, required disclosure wording, and any logo/trademark constraints.
