## 5. Known accepted risks carried into production

Recorded so they are not rediscovered as surprises.

| # | Risk | Ruling | Mitigation |
|---|------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | `DeviceRevoked` may succeed while the session cascade fails; there is no outbox | CTO 2026-07-31 — correct failure direction, device stays authoritative | Reported via the bus handler-error path; **must be routed to real monitoring** |
| 2 | Rate limiter fails **open** if Redis is unreachable | CTO 2026-07-31 — an outage must not stop sign-in | Degradation logged; alert must be routed, not merely emitted |
| 3 | Access tokens are HS256, single issuer and verifier | CTO 2026-07-31 — accepted for this topology | **Trigger:** the first service outside Identity that must verify a token → reassess and migrate to asymmetric |
| 4 | Legacy sessions predating I-7f are unbound and unaffected by device revocation | CTO 2026-07-31 | Bounded by absolute session expiry; no back-fill, no reinterpretation |
| 5 | Access-token TTL is the window between revoking a session and its tokens lapsing | Default 600 s, max 3600 s | Keep the deployed default materially below the maximum |
