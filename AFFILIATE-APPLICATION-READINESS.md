## Part 2 — Draft answers for common application questions

Copy-paste ready. Each is true on 7 August 2026 and should be re-checked before reuse.

### "Describe your website."

> HonestTotal is a pre-launch price-comparison project for US consumers. Its premise is narrow: a
> comparison is only honest if it ranks by the total you actually pay — item price plus shipping and
> unavoidable fees — rather than the headline price. The site currently publishes six original
> educational buying guides and a set of transparency documents that state the ranking method,
> the savings-verification policy and the commission-neutrality rule in full. The comparison product
> itself is not built; the site says so on every relevant page.

### "How do you promote advertisers?"

> Today, not at all — no affiliate programme has been joined and no affiliate link exists on the
> site. When one does, promotion would be through editorial content: category buying guides that
> help a reader decide, and comparison results ordered by the delivered total. There is no paid
> traffic, no coupon injection, no toolbar or extension, and no incentivised clicks. Any affiliate
> relationship would be disclosed before a hand-off, in line with the published disclosure page.

### "What type of content do you publish?"

> Original educational content written for this site. The six buying guides explain how a product
> category actually works — what the specifications mean, which ones mislead, and what to check
> before buying. They deliberately contain no prices, no product rankings, no "best" lists and no
> brand names, because we have no verified data source that would justify a recommendation. Alongside
> them we publish our operating policies: the ranking method, the four-state savings model and the
> commission-neutrality rule.

### "Who is your audience?"

> The intended audience is US consumers researching a considered electronics purchase — headphones,
> televisions, storage, laptops, keyboards — who want to know what an item will actually cost
> delivered. We have no measured audience: the site is pre-launch and has no traffic data to report.

### "How will affiliate links be used?"

> As a hand-off to the merchant's own checkout, after the buyer has been shown the all-in total and
> told that a commission may be earned. HonestTotal takes no payment and no order custody. Links
> would appear alongside comparison results and, where relevant, in buying guides. Commission is
> never an input to the ordering of results — the ranking function is not given commission data at
> all.

### "How do you generate traffic?"

> Organic search only. The site is submitted to Google Search Console with a complete sitemap, and
> traffic is expected to come from people searching for buying advice in the categories we cover.
> We do not buy traffic and do not intend to. As of today the site is **pre-launch / early
> indexing** and has no traffic figures to report.

### "Why do you want to join the network?"

> To get authorized access to merchant offer data, and a compliant way to be paid for referrals.
> The entire product depends on licensed data — the published policy is that we do not scrape and
> will not use unauthorized sources — so a network relationship is the only route to building the
> comparison at all.

### "How do you disclose affiliate relationships?"

> Through a dedicated disclosure page linked from the site, which states that we may earn commission
> on qualifying purchases and that commission never affects a recommendation. The design commitment
> is that disclosure precedes the hand-off rather than following it, and that any sponsored
> placement is labelled and kept outside the ranked results.
